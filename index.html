Capgemini Wins £37m HMRC Contract to Migrate Critical Tax System, Extending 28-Year Relationship

The contract runs until 2032, potentially extending Capgemini's continuous involvement with HMRC's core tax systems to 28 years since first winning the Aspire contract in 2004. HMRC confirmed competitive procurement via the DALAS framework, with ethical walls in place for incumbents.
