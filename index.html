Education Authority Extends Fujitsu Deal by Five Years in £15m Contract Extension

Northern Ireland's Education Authority (EA) has awarded Fujitsu a £15m extension to continue running its EA One integrated finance, HR and payroll platform until 31 March 2032, taking the total contract value to £55.4m. The deal—which covers financial accounting, HR management and teachers' payrolls for the Department of Education—was awarded without competitive tender, as EA argues the heavily customised system would create significant disruption if migrated. The extension provides useful revenue continuity for Fujitsu as it rebuilds its UK public sector business, though EA has already started a replacement programme (EnAble) targeting 2032 implementation.
