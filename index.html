Capgemini
Capgemini Secures £36m National Highways Service Management Contract
Capgemini UK has been appointed as National Highways' Service Management Maturity (SMM) partner on a five-year contract worth £35.9m (ceiling £47.2m), running from 1 July 2026 to 30 June 2031. The scope covers both IT and OT service management, awarded via the National Highways IT Commercial Framework. Notably, the contract requires Capgemini to transfer knowledge and build in-house capability within National Highways — a reflection of the growing buyer expectation for self-sufficiency in the AI era.

Home Office Splits £350m Data Analytics Deal — Deloitte Wins £200m Lot A
The Home Office has awarded Deloitte Lot A of a £350m Data Services and Analytics (DSA) framework — 'Data Led Multi-functional Teams' — capped at £200m over four years with an optional 12-month extension. Deloitte will provide integrated teams to support DSA's data strategy and standardise key data programmes. The win consolidates an already deep Home Office account and follows a recent HMRC data discovery contract, confirming Deloitte's improving momentum in UK central government.

PA Consulting Wins £150m Home Office Data Professions Contract (Lot B)

Capita
Government Admits Capita is a 'Prime Candidate' for Insourcing After Pension Chaos
A government minister told the House of Lords that Capita has 'completely failed' to restore normal service to the Civil Service Pension Scheme, after the outsourcer missed its Cabinet Office deadline of 30 June. The minister described the scheme as 'a prime candidate for future insourcing once immediate operational stability is secured' — a significant signal about Capita's long-term hold on the £239m contract. PCS union is calling for immediate insourcing, a review of the Synergy contract, and Capita to be barred from future government awards.

Fujitsu
Post Office Reassesses Horizon Replacement Bids — Signs Delayed Again to 8 July
The Post Office has formally reassessed bids for the £169.2m Horizon EPOS replacement contract and reaffirmed OneView Commerce as the winning supplier, with Escher Software losing out again. The formal contract signing has been extended to 8 July under a new standstill period — a likely supplier challenge — making it the third delay since the deal was announced in May. Fujitsu remains under contract to run Horizon until March 2027, having earned more than £2.5bn from the Post Office across 25-plus years on a system responsible for nearly 1,000 wrongful convictions.
